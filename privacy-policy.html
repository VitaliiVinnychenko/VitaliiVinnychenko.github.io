Privacy Policy

Hostena — Instagram booking assistant. Last updated: 29 July 2026.

Hostena (“the App”, “we”) is a booking assistant for a short-stay rental property. It reads direct messages sent to a connected Instagram professional account, answers questions about availability and prices, and records reservations in a connected Google Calendar.

This policy explains what data the App collects, why it is processed, how long it is kept, and how you can have it deleted. It is our own policy and applies to everyone who uses the App or messages an Instagram account connected to it.

Operator. The App is operated by [OPERATOR NAME]. For any privacy question or request, including deletion of your data, contact contact@example.com.

1. Who the App is for

There are two kinds of people whose data the App processes:

  • Hosts — the property owner or manager, who connects their own Instagram professional account and Google Calendar to the App.
  • Guests — people who send a direct message to a connected Instagram account, or who use the web chat on the host’s site.

2. Data we collect and why

Instagram account data (host)

When a host connects their Instagram professional account, the App requests the instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_manage_messages permissions and stores:

  • the Instagram account ID and username, to identify which account an incoming message belongs to;
  • a long-lived Instagram access token, encrypted at rest, so the App can receive messages and reply on the host’s behalf;
  • the token’s expiry date, so the App can refresh it before it lapses.

Direct message content (guest)

For each direct message delivered to a connected account, the App stores the message ID, the Instagram-scoped ID (IGSID) of the sender, the message text, metadata about any attachments, the timestamp, and the raw notification payload received from Meta. This is used solely to understand the guest’s request, generate a relevant reply, and keep the context of the conversation so the guest does not have to repeat themselves.

Google Calendar data (host)

If a host connects a Google account, the App stores the account’s email address, the identifier of the selected calendar, the granted scopes, and encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens. Calendar events are read to answer availability questions and written to record a confirmed reservation. The App does not read calendars other than the one the host selects.

Reservation records

When a booking is confirmed, the App stores a booking reference, the guest name given in the conversation, the cottage, check-in and check-out dates, the number of guests, the nightly and total price, and the booking status. This is what lets a guest look up their own booking later by reference.

Conversation transcripts

The App keeps a transcript of the assistant conversation (who said what, and when) so the assistant can follow a multi-step booking. Web chat conversations are keyed to a browser cookie; Instagram conversations are keyed to the sender’s IGSID.

What we do not collect

The App does not collect payment card details, government identifiers, precise location, or contact lists. It does not use your data for advertising, does not build advertising profiles, and does not sell or rent your data to anyone.

3. Legal basis for processing

Where the GDPR applies, we process host data on the basis of the host’s consent, given through the Instagram and Google authorisation screens, and guest data on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering an enquiry the guest initiated and in performing a booking the guest asked for. Consent can be withdrawn at any time, as described in section 6.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell data. We share it only with the service providers the App is built on:

  • Meta Platforms — inbound and outbound Instagram messages necessarily pass through Meta’s Instagram Messaging API.
  • Google — availability checks and reservation entries are read from and written to Google Calendar.
  • OpenAI — message text and conversation context are sent to OpenAI’s API to generate the assistant’s replies. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in a chat message.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to do so.

5. Storage, security and retention

Data is stored in a database on infrastructure controlled by the operator. Instagram and Google access tokens are encrypted at rest; they are never written to logs or exposed in the App’s interface. Access is limited to the operator.

Messages, transcripts and reservation records are kept for as long as needed to run the service and to answer questions about a past booking, and are deleted on request. When a host disconnects an account, the stored tokens for that account are removed.

6. Your rights, and how to delete your data

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you, and you can object to our processing of it. There is no charge, and we will respond within 30 days.

To request deletion, email contact@example.com from the address associated with your account, or send a message containing the words “delete my data” from the Instagram account you used. Include your Instagram username so we can locate the records. We will delete your messages, conversation transcripts and reservation records, and confirm once it is done.

To revoke the App’s access without contacting us:

  • Instagram: open Instagram, then Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, and remove the App. You can also manage this at instagram.com/accounts/manage_access.
  • Google: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove the App’s access.

Revoking access stops all further processing immediately. It does not by itself erase data already stored, so send a deletion request as well if that is what you want.

7. International transfers

The service providers listed in section 4 may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Those transfers rely on the safeguards those providers have in place, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
