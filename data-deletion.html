User Data Deletion Instructions

Hostena — Instagram booking assistant. Last updated: 29 July 2026.

You can have every piece of data Hostena holds about you deleted, at any time, for free. We handle deletion requests by email, so there is no account to log into and no form to fill in.

Send your request to contact@example.com with the subject line “Delete my data”. We confirm within 30 days, usually much sooner.

How to request deletion

  1. Email contact@example.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my data.
  3. Tell us which account to delete: your Instagram username if you messaged us on Instagram, or the Google account email you connected if you are a host. If you have a booking reference such as VY-8F3K, include it too — it helps us find every related record.
  4. Send it from an address or account we can tie to the data, so we do not delete the wrong person’s records. If you cannot, say so in the email and we will verify another way, for example by asking you to reply from the Instagram account itself.

What gets deleted

  • your direct messages, including their text, attachment metadata and the raw payloads received from Meta;
  • your conversation transcripts with the assistant;
  • your reservation records — booking reference, guest name, dates, guest count and prices;
  • for hosts: the connected Instagram and Google account details and their stored access tokens.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you had a booking, delete the data only after your stay is settled — once the records are gone we can no longer answer questions about that reservation.

What we may keep

We keep nothing for marketing or analytics. If a record must be retained to comply with a legal obligation, we will tell you which record and why, and delete it once that obligation ends. Data already held by Meta, Google or OpenAI is governed by their own policies — see the links at the end of this page.

Stopping access without deleting

If you only want the App to stop using your accounts, you can revoke its access yourself and keep your existing records:

  • Instagram: instagram.com/accounts/manage_access → remove Hostena.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove Hostena’s access.

Revoking access stops all further processing immediately, but it does not erase what is already stored. Email us as well if you want the records gone.

Other requests
